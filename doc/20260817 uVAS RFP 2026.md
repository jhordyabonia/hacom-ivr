0. ## 1\.	GENERAL

   1. ## 1.1	Background

Millicom International Cellular S.A. (“**Millicom**”) is a telecom operator in Latin America. For more information about Millicom, please visit www.millicom.com. 

As part of its ongoing network evolution and digital transformation strategy, Millicom is seeking to modernize and consolidate its Value Added Services (VAS) platforms across its operations in Chile, Uruguay, Colombia and Ecuador. 

The initiative aims to establish a unified and future-ready uVAS platform supporting messaging, voice, interactive services, and service creation capabilities while enabling the transition towards cloud-native, virtualized, and 5G-ready architectures.

Millicom is looking for a supplier capable of providing software licenses, implementation services, migration activities, integration services, support, maintenance, training, and professional services associated with the deployment of a Unified Value Added Services (uVAS) solution. 

The proposed solution shall support a converged platform including, but not limited to, SMSC, First Delivery Attempt (FDA), SMS Firewall (SMSFW), Voice Mail System (VMS), Missed Call Alert (MCA), USSD Gateway, Interactive Voice Response (IVR), Service Creation Environment (SCE) and associated charging, reporting, monitoring, and management capabilities.

This Request for Proposal (“**RFP**”) describes Millicom’s requirements for the Products and Services and on the purchasing procedure for such Products and Services. This RFP is not a commitment by Millicom to purchase the Products and Services.  
	

2. ## 1.2	Purpose of Request for Proposal

The purpose of this RFP is to:

(a)  encourage certain selected suppliers (“**Tenderers**”) to submit proposals to Millicom for the supply of Products and Services in response to this RFP (“**Proposal**”);  
(b) 	provide Tenderers with information needed to submit a Proposal;  
(c)	explain the procedure for submission of a Proposal; and   
 (d)  explain to Tenderers the evaluation criteria that will be used to assess a Proposal.

More detailed requirements for the Products and Service are set out in Annex B (Technical Requirements). 

Tenderers should read the RFP carefully to ensure they are fully familiar with the nature and extent of the obligations on Tenderers participating in the procurement process. Failure to comply with the requirements may result in a Proposal being rejected and/or a Tenderer being excluded from the procurement process.

The objective of this RFP is to select a strategic partner capable of delivering a unified, cloud-ready and future-proof uVAS solution that supports Millicom’s modernization objectives across its operations, while ensuring service continuity, operational efficiency, security, scalability, business flexibility and long-term technological evolution.
